R&R Water Refilling Station — System Guide & Master Plan

Complete operational handbook for station staff, alongside the development roadmap for upcoming hardware, receipt printing, and fleet features.

Part 1: Operational Guide

1. Walk-in Counter Sale (Instant Payment)

  1. Select 5-Gal Purified Refill (₱35.00).
  2. Tap a preset (1 to 5 Gallons) or use the 4×4 keypad.
  3. (Optional) Type the customer's name in the note input (e.g., Aling Tess).
  4. Tap Complete Sale • ₱XX.00 (Paid).
  5. Payment chime plays, transaction is logged as ✓ PAID, and cash is recorded in Drawer Cash.

2. Multicab Fleet Batch Delivery (Pay on Delivery)

  1. In the order header, switch the channel toggle to Multicab.
  2. Select batch size (10, 15, 20, 30, or 50 Gallons).
  3. Type the destination / drop point (e.g. Purok 2 - Barangay Hall).
  4. Keep the payment selector set to Pay on Delivery (Pending).
  5. Tap Dispatch Multicab • ₱XXX.00 (Pending Payment).
  6. Volume is immediately counted in station output; payment is tracked under PENDING RECEIVABLES.

3. Driver Return & 1-Click Cash Remittance

  1. When the multicab driver returns with collected money, switch to the POS Orders Log tab.
  2. Locate the delivery card marked ⏳ PENDING PAYMENT.
  3. Click the green [✓ Collect ₱XXX.XX & Remit] button.
  4. Order status updates to ✓ PAID and funds transfer into Cash in Hand (Drawer).

4. End-of-Day Shift Closeout

  1. Open the POS Orders Log.
  2. Verify all drivers have remitted (Pending Receivables should be ₱0.00).
  3. Click Export CSV to save today's official shift record.
  4. Click Start New Day / Shift to reset daily counters back to 0.
